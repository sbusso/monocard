Keys

This chapter lists the keys of Monocard. To see them in the window, press ? or choose Help > Keys (?). On a Mac, use Cmd where this guide says Ctrl. Keys that you can change are in Settings.

Move around

Key What it does
← → Previous or next photo. With Shift, adds it to the selection
↑ ↓ In the grid, the photo above or below. With Shift, adds to the selection
Home, End First or last photo. With Shift, adds to the selection
Alt+←, Alt+→ In the grid, back or forward through the folders that you visited
Ctrl+A Selects every photo shown
Ctrl+click Adds a photo to the selection, or removes it
Shift+click Selects a range. With Ctrl, adds the range

Views

Key What it does
G Library, and back
E Loupe, one photo, in Cull or Develop
D Develop
C Cull. In the Loupe, Cull or Develop in turn
L Lightbox
J Marks in the lightbox. Clipping warnings in the Loupe
V In Cull, compares two or four photos
Enter Opens the photo. From Cull, Develop
+, − Size of the tiles in the grid and the lightbox. Zoom in the Loupe
Space Fit or 1:1
Z Fit or 3:1
F A mat around the photo, at Fit
Shift+J Focus peaking
Tab Hides or shows the panes and the filmstrip
F6, F7, F8 Hides or shows the filmstrip, the left pane, or the develop panel
F11 Full screen. Not on a Mac

Monochrome views

These keys work in the Develop tab, with a monochrome photo open and no tool in hand. They cannot be changed. In this tab they replace the other uses of B, S, Z, and C.

Key What it does
A Before: the photo without your edit
B After: your edit
S Before and after, split
Z Zone view
C Clip map

Rate and sort

Key What it does
1 to 5, 0 Stars, and no stars
P, X, U Pick, reject, and no flag
6, 7, 8, 9 Red, yellow, green, and blue label. Again to clear it
[, ] Turns the photo left or right. It is not part of the edit
B Adds the selected photos to a collection, or takes them out
Ctrl+G, Ctrl+Shift+G Stacks the selected photos, or unstacks them
S In the grid, opens or closes a stack
Shift+S In the grid, puts the photo on top of its stack
Shift+[, Shift+] In the grid, moves the photo in its stack
Ctrl+' New version
Delete Deletes, after it asks. On a Mac, also ⌫

Edit

Key What it does
Ctrl+Z Undo
Ctrl+Shift+Z, Ctrl+Y Redo
Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V Copies or pastes the settings
Ctrl+Shift+S Syncs the selected photos to this one
R Crop, and back
M New mask with a Linear shape. Drag to place it
Shift+M New mask with a Radial shape. Drag to place it
[, ] Brush size, with a tool in hand
Shift+[, Shift+] Brush feather
Ctrl+[, Ctrl+] Brush opacity
O Shows the mask. In the crop, the next guide
Shift+O In the crop, turns the spiral or the triangles
\ Shows the photo as it was

Sliders

Key What it does
↑ ↓ ← →, +, − Move the slider that has the focus. Shift for more, Ctrl for less
Shift+drag Moves a slider in finer steps
, and . Previous and next slider
Tab, Shift+Tab In a typed value, the next or previous slider

Files, sheets, and help

Key What it does
Ctrl+O Opens a folder
Ctrl+F, / Filter bar
Ctrl+Shift+I Imports from a card
Ctrl+Shift+E Export
Ctrl+E Exports with the last settings
Ctrl+, Settings
? The list of keys
Esc Puts the tool down, closes the sheet, or goes back

The keys of the camera window are in The camera window.
