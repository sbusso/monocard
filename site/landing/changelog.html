0.5.1

28 November 2026

  • Fixed: Semi-stand and Stand agitation made a flat grey picture on Tri-X, T-Max 100 and 400, HP5 Plus, FP4 Plus and Delta 100, 400 and 3200. Each agitation now develops for its own normal time, and stand holds back the highlights only. At Every minute a film prints with a little more contrast than in 0.5.0, at Continuous a little less. The default, every 30 s, is unchanged.

Design note: stand development.

Release 0.5.1 on GitHub, with the checksums.

0.5.0

27 November 2026

  • Developer, Bath temperature and Agitation in FILM: D-76, D-76 1+1, HC-110 B or Rodinal 1+50, 18 to 24 °C, continuous to stand. On Tri-X, T-Max 100 and 400, HP5 Plus, FP4 Plus and Delta 100, 400 and 3200, a push or pull follows the curves of Kodak’s and Ilford’s data sheets. Development now goes from -3 to +3. An edit made in 0.4.0 on one of these eight films prints a little differently. Middle grey stays where it was.
  • Six papers: Ilford Multigrade IV RC, MG FB Classic and Multigrade Warmtone, Kodak Polymax II RC and Polycontrast IV RC, and Foma Fomatone. Split grade prints with a soft (00) and a hard (5) exposure, in stops.
  • Process: Silver gelatin, Lith, or Cyanotype.
  • Toner baths in TONING: Selenium, Sepia, Gold, Iron blue, Copper and Vanadium. Each one acts on the silver by density, as in the darkroom. Paper white stays white.
  • A film’s grain is made of the film’s silver grains, sized for a 35 mm frame across the photo. A small file no longer gets grain several times too strong.
  • Fast and Best denoise run learned models, downloaded the first time you use them. Remove JPEG blocks, in DETAIL on a JPEG, removes the 8 by 8 blocks of a compressed JPEG. It is off by default.
  • Merge burst: select 2 to 16 DNGs of one scene, shot by hand at one exposure, and merge them into one DNG with less noise.
  • A look can come from a LUT (.cube or HaldCLUT), and a look can go out as a LUT or as a Lightroom profile and preset. Twelve film looks from t3mujinpack, for color photos.
  • Export to JPEG XL, lossless 16-bit JPEG XL, and Ultra HDR.
  • The camera window imports from the camera on Linux and Windows too. On Linux, install the udev rule that comes with Monocard first.

0.4.0 opens an edit that 0.5.0 saved, without the new settings.

Design notes: denoise, development and paper, grain, LUTs and export, burst, import.

Release 0.5.0 on GitHub, with the checksums.

0.4.0

27 November 2026

  • A FILM panel, after BASIC. Choose one of 18 black and white films: Kodak Tri-X 400, T-Max 100, T-Max 400, T-Max P3200, Plus-X 125 and Double-X 5222; Ilford HP5 Plus, FP4 Plus, Pan F Plus 50, Delta 100, Delta 400, Delta 3200, SFX 200, XP2 Super and Ortho Plus 80; and Fujifilm Acros II, Neopan 400 and Neopan 1600. The photo is exposed on the film and the negative printed on Ilford Multigrade. The film’s print takes the place of the display transform.
  • Development pushes or pulls the negative, -2 to +2. Each step is a first guess, not measured.
  • Paper grade moves from BASIC to FILM. With a film, it sets the Multigrade filter. Grain moves from FINISH to FILM.
  • Each film has its own grain, from its data sheet. A fast film has large grain, a slow one fine grain. There is no grain in the paper’s white.
  • Halation: a soft glow around bright points, as light bounces off the back of the film. Edge effect: a dark and a light rim along edges, from the developer. Both 0 to 200%.
  • A color photo goes to grey through the film’s own color sensitivity. Ortho Plus 80 does not see red, so red goes dark.
  • A look, and a look for a camera mode, can hold a film and its settings.
  • Fixed: a new Filter on a color photo did not change the photo until another edit.
  • The film curves are from the makers’ data sheets, digitized by SpektraLab (CC BY-SA 4.0), and seven grain figures are from Negs (CC BY-SA 4.0). The film, print, halation and edge effect are ported from spektrafilm. See the design note.

0.3.1 opens an edit that 0.4.0 saved, without the film.

0.3.1

26 November 2026

  • The camera window keeps the Show choice, All or Not imported, for the next time.
  • The camera window closes when an import ends without a fault.
  • Start from the camera’s mode is on by default. A raw now opens with the look of its camera’s mode, as the camera’s JPEG shows it, and no longer gets darker after the preview. After an earlier version, turn it on in Settings, Editing.
  • An edited photo no longer gets darker when it opens in the loupe. Its quick picture showed the shadows too light. The quick pictures are made again once.

Release 0.3.1 on GitHub, with the checksums.

0.3.0

26 November 2026

  • Color photos open in grey. A color raw is developed with the camera’s white balance, then mixed into one grey picture. Every panel and look works on it. Color JPEG, PNG, and TIFF files too.
  • Filter, first in BASIC on a color photo: None, Yellow, Orange, Red, Green, or Blue, as the filters of black and white film. Red makes a blue sky dark. The filters are first versions, not measured.
  • Color photos show in grey in the grid. Hide color photos in Settings, Editing leaves them out, from the next start.

Release 0.3.0 on GitHub, with the checksums.

0.2.0

26 November 2026

  • A recipe book. In the grid, Import..., Recipes... keeps the camera’s picture settings. Make a recipe from a photo, compare two, import, export, and copy one as text. It does not change the camera.
  • A raw histogram, beside Histogram and Wave. It shows the sensor’s own values and how much is clipped. A JPEG has none.
  • Paper grade, under Contrast in BASIC: 00 to 5, as darkroom paper. Grade 2 changes nothing.
  • With Start from the camera’s mode on, a photo’s own settings, such as Key and Contrast, go over the look of its mode. The size of each step is a first guess, to be measured.
  • A panorama of monochrome DNGs reads the exposure and the lens of each file.

0.1.2 opens an edit that 0.2.0 saved, without the paper grade and the camera’s settings.

Release 0.2.0 on GitHub, with the checksums.

0.1.2

25 November 2026

  • The app says when a new version of Monocard is out. It asks GitHub once a day and sends only the version number. The switch is in Settings, About. It does not install the update.
  • The app and the command line show Monocard’s version and the version of Greycard that it is built on.
  • Report a problem opens Monocard’s own form.
  • A user guide, also in the Help menu.
  • A damaged DNG whose tag asks for more memory than the computer has is refused. On Linux it could end the program.
  • A Lightroom preset that you import never replaces a look with the same name.
  • Crop guides: golden ratio, golden spiral, diagonals and triangles. O steps through them.
  • Past 100%, each picture pixel is drawn as a block.
  • Panels scroll more smoothly, and a screen reader hears more of the window.
  • Built on Greycard 0.12.0 and the 191 changes after it. Greycard’s changelog has the list. Its color tools are not in Monocard.

Edits are saved in a new format. 0.1.2 reads the edits of 0.1.0 and 0.1.1. They cannot read an edit that 0.1.2 saved. To be able to go back, keep a copy of your .mcd files.

Release 0.1.2 on GitHub, with the checksums.
